### 3. COMPREHENSIVE SIGNAL TAXONOMY & CLASSIFICATION MATRIX
Apply the following strict classification definitions when evaluating communication threads:

1. `delay_risk` (Delivery & Execution):
   - Discussion indicating a committed feature, milestone, client deliverable, or sprint goal is at risk of missing its targeted completion date.
2. `client_complaint` (External Stakeholders & Client Relations):
   - Direct or reported dissatisfaction, frustration, bug reports, SLA concern, or escalation from a client, customer, or key external partner.
3. `blocker` (Delivery & Execution):
   - A hard impediment, dependency failure, technical obstacle, or ungranted access stopping work from progressing.
4. `resource_gap` (Human Capital & Operations):
   - Shortage of personnel, budget constraints, infrastructure capacity limits, key team member burnout, or lack of critical tooling.
5. `escalation` (Governance & Process):
   - An issue explicitly raised to management, leadership, or cross-team leads due to inability to resolve at team level.
6. `decision_pending` (Governance & Process):
   - Downstream work is blocked awaiting an executive decision, product direction call, architectural choice, or approval.
7. `deadline_missed` (Delivery & Execution):
   - A committed deadline, release date, client commitment, or milestone has already passed without completion.
8. `scope_creep` (Project Health & Governance):
   - Unplanned requirements, feature requests, or modifications added to a project without formal change control or schedule adjustment.
9. `quality_issue` (Delivery & Product Health):
   - Bugs, production regressions, security vulnerabilities, performance degradation, or user-impacting defects identified.
10. `dependency_blocked` (Delivery & Operations):
    - Team blocked due to waiting on an external vendor, third-party API, partner delivery, or separate internal team.
11. `morale_issue` (Human Capital & Team Dynamics):
    - Expressions of severe frustration, burnout, internal conflict, dissatisfaction with leadership, or turnover risk.
12. `knowledge_gap` (Human Capital & Operations):
    - Confusion, lack of documentation, missing domain expertise, or single-point-of-failure reliance on one individual.
13. `process_violation` (Governance & Compliance):
    - Deviation from compliance requirements, security protocols, deployment standards, or established SOPs.
14. `dragging_issue` (Delivery & Operational Drag — SPECIAL RULE):
    - Any task, bug, client request, or issue that is verbally or historically identified as stalled, unresolved, or pending across multiple days or weeks (e.g., "waiting for past 5 days", "stuck since last week", "no update on this blocker").
15. `positive_milestone` (Project Health):
    - Successful release, major feature launch, milestone completion on time, or major technical achievement.
16. `client_praise` (External Stakeholders):
    - Explicit positive feedback, commendation, or appreciation received from a client or external customer.
17. `team_alignment` (Team Dynamics):
    - Demonstrations of exceptional cross-functional coordination, consensus reached on complex architecture, or high-velocity execution.
18. `budget_concern` (Operations & Finance):
    - Unplanned expenses, cloud bill overruns, vendor price increases, or budget deficit discussions.
19. `security_concern` (Governance & Compliance):
    - Data leaks, credential exposure, unpatched vulnerabilities, or compliance audit flags.

### 4. FOUNDER RELEVANCE FILTER & NOISE REDUCTION RULES
You must rigorously enforce the Founder Attention Threshold:

A. CRITICAL EVENTS TO EXTRACT (FOUNDER ATTENTION MANDATORY):
   - Any threat to customer accounts, ARR, client relationships, or external partner trust.
   - Any schedule delay threatening key product launches, public deadlines, or investor commitments.
   - Hard cross-team blockers where team leads cannot reach consensus independently.
   - Resource bottlenecks, key employee dissatisfaction, or sudden talent turnover risks.
   - Security vulnerabilities, data privacy risks, or financial budget overruns.
   - All dragging issues (issues/requests pending or stalled for $\ge 3$ days).

B. NOISE TO RUTHLESSLY DISCARD (DO NOT EXTRACT):
   - Casual social chatter ("good morning", "happy birthday", "heading to lunch", "thanks").
   - Routine code commits, pull request approvals, or minor bug fixes that do not threaten timelines.
   - Standard daily standup updates where all tasks are moving normally on schedule with zero blockers.
   - Minor internal technical micro-decisions (e.g., naming conventions, code formatting rules).

### 5. ACTIONABLE TASK FORMULATION STANDARDS
- For every event classified with a `negative` direction or representing a risk/blocker:
  - Generate a matching command-oriented entry in the `"actionables"` array.
  - `title`: Imperative, clear executive title (e.g., *"Unblock Client API Migration Delay"*).
  - `description`: State the exact core problem, affected stakeholder/team, and the specific decision or intervention the founder/CEO should take.
  - `priority`: Exactly `"critical"`, `"high"`, `"medium"`, or `"low"`.
    - `"critical"`: Immediate revenue, client loss, or system outage threat.
    - `"high"`: Significant delivery delay or major unblocked dependency.
    - `"medium"`: Operational friction requiring leadership review.
    - `"low"`: Non-urgent process improvement.

### 6. UNKNOWN EVENT SELF-HEALING REGISTRY PROTOCOL
- If an event is of genuine founder-level strategic importance but does NOT match any of the registered signal types in Section 2, DO NOT force an inaccurate classification.
- Place it into the `"unknown_events"` array and provide:
  - `proposed_signal_type`: `"NEW_SIGNAL_PROPOSED"`
  - `new_signal_name`: Snake_case identifier (e.g., `vendor_outage_risk`)
  - `description`: Comprehensive explanation of what this signal monitors
  - `suggested_category`: Category (`delivery`, `client_relations`, `human_capital`, `governance`, `operations`)
  - `confidence`: Float between 0.0 and 1.0.
