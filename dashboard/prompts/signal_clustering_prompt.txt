### 3. CLUSTER DEFINITIONS & IMPACT MATRIX
1. `project_health` (Delivery & Execution):
   - Scope stability, milestone tracking, sprint velocity, delivery quality, architectural choices, and overall release readiness.
2. `client_relations` (External Stakeholders):
   - Client satisfaction, account retention, feedback quality, SLA adherence, complaints, support escalations, and partner trust.
3. `team_dynamics` (Human Capital):
   - Team morale, cross-functional collaboration, internal alignment, friction/conflict resolution, burnout indicators, and knowledge sharing.
4. `delivery_risk` (Risk Management):
   - Hard technical blockers, missed commitments, third-party dependency stalls, dragging bottlenecks ($\ge 3$ days), and critical path risks.
5. `process_compliance` (Governance & Control):
   - Adherence to established SOPs, security protocols, QA processes, code review standards, compliance requirements, and escalation velocity.
6. `resource_management` (Operations & Finance):
   - Staffing capacity, budget overruns, tooling/infrastructure availability, cloud cost efficiency, and talent allocation.

### 4. QUANTITATIVE CALIBRATION & SCORING PROTOCOLS

A. SIGNAL STRENGTH SCORING (-1.00 to +1.00):
   Quantify the immediate operational magnitude of the event:
   - **-0.85 to -1.00 (Critical Escalation)**: Active client churn threat, lost revenue opportunity, severe production outage, unmanaged critical blocker, dragging issue $\ge 5$ days.
   - **-0.50 to -0.84 (High Risk / Delay)**: Missed internal milestone, severe technical debt spike, key resource shortage, unhandled dependency bottleneck.
   - **-0.20 to -0.49 (Moderate Friction)**: Minor delay risk, process deviation, mild client dissatisfaction, scope ambiguity.
   - **-0.01 to -0.19 (Minor Warning)**: Informational risk warning or low-impact technical glitch.
   - **+0.10 to +0.49 (Minor Positive)**: Good progress update, minor optimization completed.
   - **+0.50 to +0.84 (High Achievement)**: Feature released on schedule, team consensus reached, positive client feedback.
   - **+0.85 to +1.00 (Major Milestone)**: Major enterprise account signed, key release deployed early with zero bugs, major strategic goal achieved.

B. RELEVANCE SCORING GUIDELINES (0.00 to 1.00):
   Quantify how critical this signal is for Founder/CEO awareness:
   - **0.80 to 1.00 (High Founder Priority)**: Any issue threatening revenue, client relationships, core product deadlines, key talent, or persisting dragging bottlenecks.
   - **0.50 to 0.79 (Moderate Leadership Priority)**: Important team process friction, architectural disagreements, moderate resource gaps.
   - **0.10 to 0.49 (Low Operational Priority)**: Standard localized technical updates or non-critical background activities.

C. CONFIDENCE EVALUATION (0.00 to 1.00):
   - Certainty score in your event-to-cluster classification based on explicit evidence in the event summary.

### 5. DYNAMIC CLUSTER PROPOSAL PROTOCOL (NEW_CLUSTER_PROPOSED)
- If an event represents a critical strategic risk domain that cannot logically be categorized under any active cluster above:
  - Assign `cluster_type: "NEW_CLUSTER_PROPOSED"`.
  - Populate the `proposed_cluster` object specifying:
    - `cluster_name`: Clean snake_case identifier (e.g., `vendor_security_compliance`).
    - `description`: Thorough definition of what this cluster monitors.
    - `category`: High-level domain (`delivery`, `client_relations`, `human_capital`, `governance`, `operations`).
    - `persistence`: Float between 0.10 and 1.00 (how long risks in this domain persist).
    - `decay_rate`: Float between 0.001 and 0.100 (daily mathematical decay rate).
