### 4. RIGOROUS CLUSTER SCORING & AUDIT DIRECTIVES

A. CLUSTER HEALTH SCORE SCALE (Integer 0 to 100):
   Evaluate all signals belonging to each specific cluster. Factor in signal magnitude (`strength`), `relevance_score`, `confidence`, and time-decay:
   - **80 to 100 ("Healthy")**:
     - Operations executing smoothly at high velocity.
     - Minimal isolated minor risks; positive milestone momentum.
     - Zero unresolved blockers or client escalations.
   - **60 to 79 ("Stable")**:
     - Operations stable with manageable routine friction.
     - Minor technical debt or non-critical process ambiguity present.
     - No active delivery blockages or client churn threats.
   - **40 to 59 ("Warning")**:
     - Moderate operational drag and risk accumulation.
     - Active unresolved blockers, scope creep, or client complaints requiring leadership monitoring.
     - 1-2 dragging bottlenecks pending across multiple days.
   - **0 to 39 ("Critical")**:
     - Severe operational emergency requiring immediate Founder/CEO intervention.
     - Critical client churn risk, missed committed release deadlines, key talent turnover, or multiple persistent dragging bottlenecks ($\ge 5$ days unresolved).

B. STATUS & TREND CLASSIFICATION DIRECTIVES:
   - `"status"`: Must strictly align with the health score:
     - `"Healthy"` ($\ge 70$)
     - `"Stable"` ($50 - 69$)
     - `"Warning"` ($30 - 49$)
     - `"Critical"` ($< 30$)
   - `"confidence"`: Float between 0.00 and 1.00 reflecting data density and clarity of signals.
   - `"summary"`: Write a concise, authoritative 2-3 sentence executive audit briefing explaining exactly why this cluster received this score. Call out specific high-impact risks, client accounts, or achievements driving the assessment.

C. UNMONITORED CLUSTER DEFAULTING RULE:
   - If an active cluster has zero signals present in the input telemetry feed, default its parameters to:
     - `health_score`: 50
     - `status`: `"Stable"`
     - `confidence`: 1.00
     - `summary`: `"No operational signals recorded for this cluster during the current ingestion cycle. Operating at baseline status."`
